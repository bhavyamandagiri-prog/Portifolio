About Me

The background, vision, and core skillset driving my academic and professional journey.

Transforming Ideas Into Silicon & Software

I am a 3rd-year B.Tech Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) student at Andhra Loyola Institute of Engineering and Technology (ALIET), affiliated with JNTUK. My training focuses on VLSI systems and digital electronics, which I combine with modern full-stack web development and artificial intelligence models.

I specialize in designing VLSI circuits, coding full-stack web applications, and building AI models. I am highly interested in VLSI DFT (Design for Testability), software architecture, and AI integrations, aiming to create innovative tech solutions for top-tier companies.

"I believe the future of computing lies at the convergence of smart systems and scalable web infrastructures. My drive is to build technologies that feel invisible but make the world work better."
